# G-SIFI Practitioner Guide: Systemic AGI Governance
*Focus: Global Systemically Important Financial Institutions (G-SIFIs)*

## 1. Regulatory Context (SR 11-7 & NIST RMF)
For G-SIFIs, AGI governance is an extension of Model Risk Management (MRM).
- **SR 11-7 Compliance**: Recursive agents must be treated as "Living Models" with continuous validation.
- **NIST AI RMF**: Mapping, Measuring, and Managing systemic risks.

## 2. The 7 Pillars of Systemic Governance
1. **Algorithmic Solvency**: Ensuring AI-driven trading/risk models do not trigger liquidity cascades.
2. **Recursive Auditability**: Sidecar-based logging (Kafka WORM) for agentic decision trails.
3. **Model Risk Registry**: Centralized inventory of all LLM/AGI deployments.
4. **Ethics Review Board (ERB)**: Oversight of bias, fairness, and safety alignment.
5. **Data Provenance**: Immutable tracking of training data and fine-tuning sets.
6. **Red-Teaming & Stress Testing**: Periodic adversarial simulations for "flash crash" scenarios.
7. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Controls**: Defined escalation paths for high-value transactions.

## 3. Deployment Specifications
- **Level 1 (Foundational)**: Standard LLM for internal productivity.
- **Level 2 (Agentic)**: Semi-autonomous workflow orchestrators.
- **Level 3 (Autonomous)**: ASI-ready systemic controllers (Requires Tier 3 Oversight).
