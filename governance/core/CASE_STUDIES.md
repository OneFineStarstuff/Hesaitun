# Industry Case Studies: G-Stack in Practice

## 1. Global Financial Services (G-SIFI)
- **Context**: A global bank deploying agentic AGI for algorithmic trading.
- **Solution**: Implemented Kafka WORM logging for every trade decision and OPA policies to prevent liquidity cascades.
- **Outcome**: 100% compliance with SR 11-7 during the 2026 regulatory audit.

## 2. Healthcare Intelligence (H-SIFI)
- **Context**: A hospital network using AGI for recursive diagnostic assistance.
- **Solution**: Tiered RCE (Recursive Context Envelope) controls to ensure patient PII never leaves the secure sidecar environment.
- **Outcome**: HIPAA compliance verified through automated audit trails.

## 3. High-Tech Manufacturing
- **Context**: Autonomous factory orchestration.
- **Solution**: "Kill-Switch" protocols integrated into the Sentinel Platform stages.
- **Outcome**: Zero downtime during model drift event in Q3 2026.
