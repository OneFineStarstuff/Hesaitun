# Governance Architecture: The 3-Tiered Model

## Overview
Effective AGI governance requires a hierarchical structure that balances strategic oversight with operational agility. The G-Stack Framework utilizes a 3-tiered organizational design.

## Tier 1: Strategic Oversight (Board Level)
- **Body**: Board AI Committee.
- **Responsibility**: Fiduciary oversight, ethical mandate setting, and high-level risk appetite.
- **Inputs**: Board Handout, Executive Dashboards.

## Tier 2: Operational Governance (Executive Level)
- **Body**: Executive AI Risk Committee.
- **Responsibility**: Policy enforcement, resource allocation, and incident response management.
- **Leadership**: Chief AI Officer (CAIO).

## Tier 3: Technical Implementation (Center of Excellence)
- **Body**: AI Center of Excellence (CoE).
- **Responsibility**: Compliance-as-code deployment, real-time monitoring, and business unit support.
- **Tools**: OPA Policy Engines, Kafka WORM Logging.
