# Communication Playbook: Managing the AGI Narrative

## 1. Principles of Precision
- **Accuracy over Assurance**: Never downplay risks to gain comfort.
- **Continuous Cadence**: Regular updates prevent "information vacuums" where fear grows.
- **Dual-Track Communication**: Technical depth for developers, strategic clarity for leaders.

## 2. Echo Mapping & Drift Management
- **The Echo Map**: Tracking how governance messages are repeated across the org.
- **Counter-Echo Strategy**: Identifying and correcting misinterpretations of safety protocols before they become "shadow policy."

## 3. Crisis Protocols
- **Immediate (0-4h)**: Transparency about the incident; "Stay in Control" messaging.
- **Secondary (4-24h)**: Technical root cause analysis for stakeholders.
- **Recovery (24h+)**: Policy refinement and "Lessons Learned" Board Brief.
