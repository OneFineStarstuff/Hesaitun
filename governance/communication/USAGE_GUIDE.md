# Usage Guide: Integrating Governance into Daily Routines

## For the CEO/Board
- **Frequency**: Quarterly.
- **Tool**: Executive Dashboard & 4-Quadrant Handout.
- **Action**: Strategic sign-off and resource allocation.

## For the CAIO/CoE
- **Frequency**: Continuous/Weekly.
- **Tool**: 18-Component Maturity Radar.
- **Action**: Operational oversight and policy refinement.

## For Engineering/DevOps
- **Frequency**: Sprint-based.
- **Tool**: OPA Policy Engine & Kafka Audit Logs.
- **Action**: Implementing "Compliance-as-Code".

## For Legal/Risk
- **Frequency**: Monthly.
- **Tool**: Regulatory Compliance Master & Model Registry.
- **Action**: Verification and audit reporting.

---
*Goal: Zero-friction integration into existing agile/strategic workflows.*
