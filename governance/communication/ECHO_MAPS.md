# Boardroom Echo Maps & Memory Trace Matrices

## 1. Visual Rhythm Map
- **Concept**: Mapping the frequency and "beat" of governance messaging to ensure optimal retention without "fatigue."
- **Metric**: High-impact messaging vs. passive updates.

## 2. Boardroom Echo Map
- **Concept**: A visual representation of how key governance decisions (the "Sound") are reflected in business unit actions (the "Echo").
- **Goal**: Minimize the "Lag Time" between policy and implementation.

## 3. Counter-Echo Map
- **Concept**: Identifying "Resistance Nodes" where policy is being ignored or misinterpreted.
- **Action**: Targeted intervention for "Muted" business units.

## 4. Memory Trace Matrix
- **Concept**: Tracking long-term retention of core safety principles among leadership.
- **Method**: Surveys and "Spot-Checks" during strategic planning.
