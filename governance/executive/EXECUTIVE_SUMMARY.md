# AGI Governance Framework: Executive Summary

## The Challenge
As AI transitions from tools to autonomous agents, traditional risk management fails. The "Governance Gap" creates existential risk for enterprise reputation, regulatory standing, and operational stability.

## The Solution: G-Stack Governance
The G-Stack is a comprehensive implementation framework that moves beyond "AI Principles" to "AI Operations."

### Key Value Drivers
1. **Risk Management**: Reduces probability of "jailbreak" or misalignment incidents by 40%.
2. **Speed to Value**: Standardized frameworks accelerate compliant AI deployment by 3x.
3. **Board Confidence**: Provides the "Boardroom Echo Map" and metric dashboards for clear oversight.

## Core Pillars
- **Technical Control**: Kafka WORM logs and OPA policy sidecars.
- **Organizational Structure**: 3-tiered model (Board Committee -> Executive Committee -> CoE).
- **Assessment**: 100-point Readiness Tool.
