# Sentinel Governance Platform: 10-Stage Evolution

## Overview
The Sentinel Platform is the long-term technical roadmap for managing the transition from LLMs to Superintelligence (ASI).

## Stages 1-4: Foundational LLMs
- Focus on bias, toxicity, and basic RAG governance.

## Stages 5-7: Agentic AGI
- Focus on agency, planning, and goal-alignment.
- Implementation of "Recursive Context Envelopes" (RCE).

## Stages 8-10: Superintelligent ASI
- Focus on civilizational safety and global registry synchronization.
- Deployment of the "Luminous Engine" for automated alignment.
