# AI Center of Excellence (CoE) Framework

## Mission
To serve as the central repository of AI governance expertise and provide operational oversight for all AI initiatives.

## Core Functions
1. **Consulting**: Advising business units on compliant AI deployment.
2. **Monitoring**: Real-time auditing via the Technical Sidecars.
3. **Education**: Developing the "Practitioner's Mindset" across the org.
4. **Standardization**: Maintaining the "Master Reference" library.

## Organizational Design
- **Lead**: CAIO (Chief AI Officer).
- **Staffing**: Data Scientists, Ethics Specialists, Legal Counsel, InfoSec.
- **Reporting**: Directly to the Executive AI Risk Committee.

---
*Operating Model: Federated (Central oversight with BU implementation).*
