# The 18-Component Governance Model

## 1. Foundation & Ethics
- **Ethics Review**: Alignment with human values.
- **Transparency**: Model explainability protocols.
- **Accountability**: Defined liability structures.

## 2. Technical Operations
- **Model Risk Management**: Version control and validation.
- **Data Governance**: Provenance and privacy tracking.
- **Security & Safety**: Red-teaming and containment.

## 3. Human & Organizational
- **Skill Development**: Literacy for non-technical leaders.
- **Center of Excellence**: Internal consulting/oversight body.
- **Change Management**: Transitioning to agentic workflows.

## 4. Advanced Containment
- **Kill-Switch Protocols**: Emergency shutdown procedures.
- **Recursive Monitoring**: Sidecar-based audit trails.
- **Alignment Verification**: Automated consistency checks.

*(Full list includes: Bias Mitigation, Data Privacy, Third-Party Risk, Legal Compliance, Cultural Persistence, etc.)*
