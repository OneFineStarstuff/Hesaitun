# Governance Readiness Assessment Tool (v1.0)

## Overview
A 100-point maturity scale designed to baseline organizational readiness for AGI deployment.

## Scoring Categories
1. **Leadership & Strategy (20 pts)**: Presence of CAIO, Board awareness, budget allocation.
2. **Policy & Compliance (20 pts)**: AI Ethics Policy, Regulatory mapping, Legal review.
3. **Technical Controls (20 pts)**: Audit logging (WORM), Monitoring sidecars, Model registry.
4. **Data & Privacy (20 pts)**: Provenance tracking, Synthetic data usage, Privacy-preserving tech.
5. **Organizational Culture (20 pts)**: Literacy programs, Incident response plans, Ethical incentives.

## Maturity Levels
- **0-20: Reactive**: No formal strategy; ad-hoc AI usage.
- **21-40: Defensive**: Compliance-focused; siloed governance.
- **41-60: Proactive**: Standardized frameworks; central CoE.
- **61-80: Optimized**: Continuous monitoring; real-time dashboards.
- **81-100: Sentinel-Ready**: ASI-capable governance; recursive alignment.
